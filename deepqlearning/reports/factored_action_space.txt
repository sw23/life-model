Factored standing-policy actions (Plan 32 D8/D9) - PPO on financial:basic, retirement_security
=================================================================================================

Action space. MultiDiscrete nvec = [7, 7, 4, 3, 4, 6, 5, 3, 3, 4]: ten slots (pretax_401k_rate,
roth_401k_rate, ira_contribution, hsa_contribution, sweep_policy, spending_adjust, withdrawal_order,
retire, claim_social_security, roth_conversion), index 0 = NO_CHANGE in every slot, 46 mask entries.
The joint space is 2,540,160 actions, so FlattenPolicies(mode="auto") uses capped sequential sub-steps
(Discrete(37): commit + 36 (slot, value) pairs, at most 3 changes per decision) for DQN. PPO consumes the
MultiDiscrete space natively (one masked categorical head per slot). The flat Discrete(52) space stays
the env default.

Runs (seeds 0-4 pre-registered; protocol n=200 per condition, master_seed 12345, factored baselines):
  year:    python -m deepqlearning.train --env financial:basic --algo ppo --action-space factored \
             --decision-interval year --total-env-steps 200000 --num-envs 8 \
             --reward-preset retirement_security --protocol-eval --protocol-n-eval 200 --seed N
  quarter: same with --decision-interval quarter --total-env-steps 800000 --set gamma=0.9975 \
             --set gae_lambda=0.987 --set n_steps=512 --set train.eval_freq_steps=20000
Per-seed results and the pooled verdicts are in algorithm_sweep.txt; seed 0's protocol report, pooled
report and analysis are in factored_ppo_year/ and factored_ppo_quarter/.

Pooled paired gap to the best factored heuristic (contribution_waterfall), train condition, t95 over seeds:
  factored PPO, yearly decisions:    -0.66 [-1.56, +0.24]  (1/5 runs above the bar on mean)
  factored PPO, quarterly decisions: -0.79 [-2.65, +1.08]  (2/5)
For comparison on the same engine and seeds (flat space, bar = always_max_401k 35.30):
  flat PPO:  -0.57 [-1.37, +0.22]  (0/5)        flat DQN: +0.19 [+0.01, +0.38]  (4/5)
At yearly decisions returns are comparable across the spaces: the best factored heuristic scores 35.31 and
the best flat one 35.30; mean agent return over the five seeds is 34.64 for factored PPO, 34.72 for flat
PPO and 35.49 for flat DQN. Quarterly returns are on their own scale (months/12 weighting; accrual stops at
the death quarter) and are compared only with the quarterly heuristics (bar 34.94).

decisions holding it when below 100%; full tables in policy_vector_by_age.txt). "age" is the env's
decision age (the age during the period just simulated) and the vector is the one in force after that
decision, i.e. going into the next period: row 64 shows the household retired in the January it turns 65.

Reading. The yearly agent defers 15% of pay pre-tax and 15% Roth (30% in all, inside the 402(g) limit),
funds a traditional IRA to the limit from the bank, runs a standing conversion to the top of the 22%
bracket while working, holds cash (no sweep), claims Social Security when it retires at the household's
age (65) and switches the waterfall to taxable-then-pre-tax-then-Roth (brokerage_first) in retirement. It
matches the contribution-waterfall heuristic on the train seeds (paired gap +0.00 [-0.00, +0.01]) with a
slightly higher median estate. The quarterly agent instead defers 15% Roth and nothing pre-tax, funds the
traditional IRA, sweeps surplus cash into the bond fund from its 50s, and claims at retirement; it beats the
quarterly bar on mean (+0.22) but with a 2.5% ruin rate and a wide CI. Neither verdict is INTELLIGENT, and
two of the five yearly seeds and one quarterly seed are well below the bar.

Default. The factored space stays opt-in: on the same engine, seeds and protocol, factored PPO does not do
better than flat PPO (pooled -0.66 vs -0.57; per-seed spread is wider) and neither beats its bar, while flat
DQN is the only configuration whose pooled interval clears zero. The factored space's gains are
interpretability (standing policies a planner recognizes, reported per year) and the adviser/spec
vocabulary, not return; switching the default would also orphan the committed flat configs and reports.
